3 · Links between slices — where plant behavior emerges

  • Handoff rule (every link, every tick): available = min(upstream output, buffer stock); actual = min(capacity, available); the difference accumulates as a queue on the link.
  • Congestion propagates upstream: queue > buffer_max → upstream hard-capped to downstream capacity + congestion flag. A jammed conveyor throttles the unloader three stages back.
  • Buffers decouple rhythms: DRP continuous vs EAF batch — the DRI buffer exists so the furnace never starves; billet yard sold to the manager as days of casting cover.
  • Governing constraint (SME rule): the caster caps the whole meltshop regardless of furnace power.
  • The heat is the only discrete object crossing boundaries: {size, DRI share, Cu/Sn tramp, grade, status}. Chemistry rides the graph from blend to product — quality is carried, not asserted at the end.

4 · Mass balance — the contract

Every stage: Inputs = Products + By-products + Losses + ΔInventory, with named accumulators matching a real mill balance sheet.

StageLoss accumulator
DRPdust/fines + unreduced FeO → yield_loss
EAFphysical handling loss (separate, SME rule) + oxidation/melt yield loss
CCM / millsscale + crop/cobble → yield_loss · yield chain: EAF yield → CCM 96–98% → mill yield
Exitsold (dispatch) · dumped (100% loss). Slag/dust live as losses until sustainability converts them to products.

Regression gate: material-balance error asserted at 0.0000 t (tolerance 1e-6/accumulator — float noise only). Python mirror + TypeScript port in parity tests: two independent implementations must agree on every tick.

5 · Throughput, bottleneck & cost math (per node, per tick)

available   = min(upstream_output_tph, buffer_remaining)
actual      = min(capacity_tph, available)
queue      += upstream_output − actual
bottleneck  : capacity_tph < upstream_output_tph
hard cap    : queue > buffer_max → throttle upstream + congestion flag

opex(N)     = actual_throughput × opex_per_t × energy_multiplier
energy_mult(EAF) = 1 + hold_pct/100 + aging_pct/100
   hold  : tiered by ladle hours held (float-safe lookup)
   aging : DRI stockpile age > 30 days
revenue     : dispatch outflow × grade price ladder — buffers cannot hide a money problem
